# How to open the storefront

You do not need Git, coding experience, Shopify, Node.js, or a terminal for the easiest review method.

## Windows

1. Open this repository on GitHub.
2. Click the green **Code** button.
3. Click **Download ZIP**.
4. Open your Downloads folder.
5. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose **Extract All**.
6. Open the extracted `fousey` folder.
7. Double-click `index.html`.
8. Your normal web browser will open the storefront.

## Mac

1. Open this repository on GitHub.
2. Click the green **Code** button.
3. Click **Download ZIP**.
4. Open your Downloads folder.
5. Double-click the ZIP if macOS has not extracted it automatically.
6. Open the extracted `fousey` folder.
7. Double-click `index.html`.
8. Safari, Chrome, or your default browser will open the storefront.

## What to test

- Resize the browser or open it on a phone-sized screen.
- Click each product finish color.
- Increase/decrease quantity.
- Click **Add to bag**.
- Open and close the cart drawer.
- Scroll on mobile size and watch the sticky purchase bar appear.
- Open the FAQ items.

## What this proves

This is a design and implementation study, not a fake client project. The fictional product lets a reviewer inspect the front-end quality without needing access to a real client's private assets.

The same structure can be adapted to Shopify with real product photos/video, copy, variants, theme settings, Shopify cart behavior, and native checkout.

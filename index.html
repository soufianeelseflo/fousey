03 / QUESTIONS

Answer doubt before it becomes a bounce.

Is this a real product?

No. FORM 01 is a fictional storefront study created to demonstrate design and implementation quality without pretending it is client work.

Can this be implemented in Shopify?

Yes. The page structure maps cleanly to Shopify sections, native product variants, the product form, cart drawer logic, theme settings and standard checkout handoff.

Can real assets replace the mock product?

Yes. Real photography, copy, product data, brand fonts, UGC and reference material can replace the study content while preserving the interaction and conversion architecture.

Does it work without JavaScript?

The core page remains readable. JavaScript enhances color selection, quantity controls, cart behavior, sticky purchase UI and motion.
